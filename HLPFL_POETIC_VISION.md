## Origin Story

**Founded**: 2019  
**Location**: Grand Rapids, Michigan  
**Founder**: James Rockel (at age 18)  
**Co-Founder**: Alki  

James Rockel founded HLPFL at age 18 with a revolutionary vision: to break the cages that record labels built and give musicians wings, not chains or guilt. Born from personal experience of being used and burned by the traditional music industry, James transformed his pain into purpose.

Starting as a personal mission to protect creatives from exploitation, HLPFL has grown from a small local initiative to a global movement that revolutionizes how the music industry operates. The platform provides artists with tools and freedom instead of contracts and masters.
