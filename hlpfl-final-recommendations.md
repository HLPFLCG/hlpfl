# HLPFL - Final Strategic Recommendations & Action Plan

## 🎯 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY - CORRECTED MODEL

**HLPFL is an artist management company, NOT a record label.**

**Core Business Model:**
- 11% commission on artist net revenue
- Non-exclusive, artist-friendly management agreements
- Artists retain 89% of income and 100% ownership
- 12-month terms with easy 30-day termination
- Full financial transparency and audit rights

**Dual Revenue Strategy:**
1. **Management Services (40% of revenue)** - 11% commission from managed artists
2. **Technology Platform (45% of revenue)** - BioBetter subscriptions for independent artists
3. **Additional Services (15% of revenue)** - Consulting, education, partnerships

## 🚨 WHAT NOT TO DO

### Avoid These Mistakes:

❌ **Don't position as a record label**
- You're a management company, not a label
- Don't talk about "signing" artists (use "partnering with")
- Don't mention owning masters or controlling releases

❌ **Don't hide the 11% commission**
- Be transparent about commission structure
- Show calculations clearly
- Emphasize artist keeps 89%

❌ **Don't lock artists into long-term contracts**
- Keep 12-month terms with easy termination
- Emphasize non-exclusive nature
- Make termination process simple and clear

❌ **Don't try to control creative decisions**
- Artists have full creative control
- You advise, they decide
- No signing authority or power of attorney

❌ **Don't overpromise on revenue projections**
- Be realistic about growth timelines
- Focus on sustainable growth
- Emphasize long-term partnership over quick wins
