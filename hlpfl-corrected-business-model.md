**ACTUAL BUSINESS MODEL:**

## 📋 BUSINESS MODEL ANALYSIS

### Artist Management Agreement Key Terms

**Commission Structure:**
- **11% flat commission** on Net Revenue only
- **Net Revenue = Gross Income - Approved Business Expenses**
- Approved expenses include:
  - Recording costs
  - Touring expenses
  - Marketing costs
  - Legal fees
  - Agent commissions (up to 11%)
  - Other artist-approved expenses

**Artist Protections:**
- ✅ Non-exclusive agreement
- ✅ Artist can terminate with 30 days notice
- ✅ No commission on pre-existing deals
- ✅ No commission after agreement ends
- ✅ Artist receives ALL money first
- ✅ Full creative control retained by artist
- ✅ Artist owns all masters, publishing, merchandise
- ✅ No power of attorney or signing authority for manager

**Agreement Terms:**
- 12-month initial term
- Renewal requires artist's written consent
- Artist can audit books anytime
- Quarterly financial statements required
- Manager has fiduciary duty to artist
