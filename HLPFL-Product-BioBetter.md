# Product: BioBetter

## Overview
BioBetter is an all-in-one artist platform that combines link-in-bio management, social media scheduling, streaming analytics, and monetization tools in a single affordable subscription. It provides independent artists with professional-grade tools at a fraction of the cost of competitors like Linktree Pro ($24/month) or Later ($40/month).

## Target Customer
**Primary:** Independent musicians and artists earning $10K-$100K annually who need professional tools to manage their online presence, grow their audience, and monetize their work without expensive subscriptions.

**Secondary:** Emerging artists and content creators who are building their careers and need affordable, easy-to-use tools to consolidate their links, schedule content, and track performance across platforms.

## Problem It Solves
Independent artists face a fragmented and expensive technology landscape. They need Linktree for their bio link ($24/month), Later or Hootsuite for social media scheduling ($40-$99/month), separate analytics tools for streaming platforms, and additional services for monetization. This adds up to $100-$200/month in subscriptions, which is prohibitive for emerging artists.

## Pricing

### Free Tier - $0/month
**Includes:**
- Basic link-in-bio page with up to 10 links
- BioBetter branding on page
- Basic click analytics

**Best For:** Emerging artists just starting out

### Pro Tier - $9.99/month
**Includes:**
- Everything in Free Tier
- Unlimited links on bio page
- Remove BioBetter branding
- Custom themes and full customization
- Connect up to 3 social media accounts
- Schedule up to 30 posts per month

**Best For:** Independent artists with growing audiences

### Business Tier - $29.99/month
**Includes:**
- Everything in Pro Tier
- Unlimited social media accounts
- Unlimited scheduled posts
- AI-powered content suggestions
- All streaming platforms connected
- Advanced analytics and reporting

**Best For:** Professional independent artists managing active careers
